About the Suburb

About Hendra

Hendra is a sought-after inner-northern Brisbane suburb known for its leafy streets, proximity to the airport, and family-friendly atmosphere. With excellent transport links and a blend of character homes and modern developments, Hendra offers the perfect balance of convenience and community.

Lifestyle

Quiet residential streets lined with established trees, weekend markets, and a strong community spirit. Cafés, restaurants, and the Racecourse Road precinct offer vibrant dining just minutes from home.

Schools

Within the catchment for Hendra State School and Eagle Junction State School, with St Agatha's, Kedron State High, Brisbane Boys' College and Somerville House all within easy reach.

Public Transport

Hendra railway station is within walking distance, with multiple bus routes and direct access to the Airport Link tunnel. Brisbane CBD is just 10 minutes by train.

Shopping

The Racecourse Road precinct, Toombul Shopping Centre, Clayfield Markets, Portside Wharf, and DFO Brisbane Airport are all within a short drive.

Parks & Recreation

Kalinga Park, Doomben and Eagle Farm racecourses, plus extensive walking and cycling paths along the Kedron Brook. Multiple playing fields and sporting clubs cater to all ages.

Population

Approximately 4,500 residents, predominantly families and professionals, with a median age of 38. The suburb has seen steady population growth over the past decade.

Demographics

Mix of young families, established professionals and downsizers. High home-ownership rate with median household income comfortably above the national average.
