RP Prospects 9 / Suburb
Chapter Eight

The Suburb

“A thriving community with all the essentials of modern living at your doorstep.”

About Hendra

Hendra is a sought-after inner-northern Brisbane suburb known for its leafy streets, proximity to the airport, and family-friendly atmosphere.

Lifestyle

Quiet residential streets, weekend markets, and a strong community spirit. The Racecourse Road precinct offers vibrant dining.

Schools

Within catchment for Hendra & Eagle Junction state schools, with Brisbane Boys' College and Somerville House nearby.

Public Transport

Hendra railway station within walking distance. Brisbane CBD is just 10 minutes by train with direct Airport Link access.

Shopping

Racecourse Road, Toombul Shopping Centre, Clayfield Markets, Portside Wharf and DFO Brisbane Airport.

Parks & Recreation

Kalinga Park, Doomben and Eagle Farm racecourses, plus walking and cycling paths along the Kedron Brook.

Population

Approximately 4,500 residents, predominantly families and professionals, with a median age of 38.

Demographics

Mix of young families, established professionals and downsizers. High home-ownership rate, above-average incomes.
